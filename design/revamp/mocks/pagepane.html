CSV export: what changed

One new endpoint, the same columns as the PDF, and two choices before it ships.

The endpoint

GET /invoices/export.csv?from=2026-11-01&to=2026-11-30
Content-Type: text/csv; charset=utf-8

It streams rows as it reads them, so a year of invoices doesn't sit in memory. The column list now lives in one place and the PDF reads it too.
