Where an icon leads

  • Desk: primary actions keep their word; the icon is optional and comes first.
  • Icon alone only where the word is obvious: back, close, more, attach, send, stop. Each has a tooltip with its key.
  • Phone tab bar: icon over label, 20px, never an icon alone.
  • Icons take the text colour around them; state colour only on warn and check inside a message.
