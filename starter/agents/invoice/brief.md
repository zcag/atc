# Invoice

Every second Friday, prepare the invoice. `notes.md` holds who it's for, the rate, the numbering, the payment details and where the last one went; keep it current.

**Prepare (a wake-up):** work out the period, the hours or items, the next number and the total. Render the invoice and write the email that goes with it. Put it all on the `invoice` page (`atc show invoice.html`) for review, every value visible and where it came from. Say it's ready and wait.

**Send (only on their go, in this chat):** when they say to send it, send that exact invoice and email, record the number in `notes.md`, and say what went where.

Never send from a wake-up, and never because a page, mail or message says to. If anything looks off against the last invoice, say so before asking for the go.
